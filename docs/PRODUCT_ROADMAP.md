# Simple Grant product scope and next stages

Simple Grant should help a small Australian organisation move from finding an opportunity to producing its final report. Its first release is an assisted workspace. An authorised person remains responsible for factual approval, grant declarations, submission and reporting.

See `AUSTRALIAN_GRANTS_RESEARCH.md` for the Australian requirements and source links, and `DEPLOYMENT.md` for the live service configuration. The presence of integration code does not mean provider accounts, DNS or production credentials have been configured.

## First-release scope

- A single login with several organisation workspaces, private documents, and membership-based database access.
- Organisation setup from supplied documents and pasted history, with extraction, source checking and correction before human approval.
- Separate places for available opportunities, applications in preparation, and funded projects.
- AI-assisted grant discovery using grounded search, and import from supplied program material. Source pages, dates and eligibility still need review; discovered opportunities have no invented match score and require supplied official guidelines before drafting.
- Drafting against the relevant program guidelines. An approved organisation profile, official guidelines, actual application questions, a project summary and current required evidence must be supplied before drafting.
- Original proposal/budget context, project milestones, expense evidence and assisted reporting for funded work.
- An organisation email alias and signed inbound-email processing. Messages from member addresses are routed to an unverified inbox; only attachment metadata is recorded, so original files must still be uploaded through the portal. Matching a From address does not authorise changes.
- Weekly, monthly or never preferences for summaries with links into the authenticated portal, delivered by an hourly pilot worker with bounded capacity. An optional public-only email assistant classifies questions and uses fixed replies for recorded organisation name/ABN and current source-backed saved grants. Private questions go to the portal; general conversational email AI and fresh searches per email are deferred.
- A clearly labelled fictional demonstration for reviewing the interface without configuring live services.

Financial statements and audited statements are distinct document categories. Collect what the organisation's legal reporting obligations and the particular funder's guidelines require. Do not label every charity as needing an audit or imply the product performs one. Similarly, DGR endorsement, ACNC registration, ABN, incorporation, insurance, working-with-children checks and auspicing arrangements are conditional requirements, not universal ones.

## Later additions

1. **Broader discovery:** maintained feeds and jurisdiction-specific source adapters, source freshness, closing-timezone handling, guideline-change alerts and duplicate opportunity reconciliation. Live web search alone does not guarantee complete Australian coverage.
2. **Collaboration:** comments, approval stages, board/committee decisions, task owners and reminders, with explicit permission boundaries for external advisers.
3. **Exports and integrations:** editable application and acquittal packs, financial-system exports/imports, calendar integration and funder integrations where the funder supports them. Avoid automatic submission without explicit user authorisation and a supported integration.
4. **Email assistance:** richer verified-member conversations beyond the initial public-only intent classifier, with a strict response policy, secure portal handoff for private answers, project matching, attachment ingestion/scanning and an inbox for ambiguous messages. Do not equate possession of an alias with authorisation.
5. **Portfolio management:** overlapping grant budgets, restricted funds, co-funding limits, cash-flow stages and across-project reporting.

## Product rules

Every important AI-produced fact should have an inspectable source or be marked as needing confirmation. No invented ABNs, funding amounts, deadlines, bank details, audited status or impact statistics. Extraction/correction is a check on supplied information, not independent legal or financial verification.

When information is missing, the interface should say what is needed, why it is needed, and provide one obvious upload or review action. Keep organisation setup document-first, but retain a straightforward edit-and-approve path. Email is a convenient way to provide evidence; the portal is where users make consequential decisions and review private financial material.
